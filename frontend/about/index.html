About VidSnatch

VidSnatch is a web-based media utility designed to make it simple to process supported, publicly accessible media URLs from compatible platforms.

What VidSnatch does

VidSnatch provides a browser-based interface where a user can submit a supported public media URL, retrieve available media information, and choose an available download format when the service and source permit it.

Responsible use

Use VidSnatch only for content you own, have permission to use, or are otherwise legally permitted to download. Users are responsible for complying with applicable laws, copyright requirements, and the rules of the platform where the content was published.

VidSnatch is not affiliated with YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X, Reddit, Threads, Pinterest, or Snapchat unless expressly stated.

Privacy-focused approach

VidSnatch does not require an account to use the downloader interface. For information about submitted URLs, temporary processing, cookies, and advertising-related data, see our Privacy Policy.
