Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VidSnatch handles information when you use the VidSnatch website and its media-processing features.

Information you provide

When you use the downloader, you may submit a public media URL. That URL is processed to retrieve media information and, when supported, prepare the requested download. Do not submit passwords, authentication tokens, private account information, or other sensitive information.

Technical information

Like most websites and hosting infrastructure, requests may be processed by web servers, hosting providers, security systems, or network services that can receive standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, timestamps, and request details. VidSnatch does not ask users to create an account for the downloader.

Temporary processing

Media information and download jobs may be processed temporarily in order to complete a request. Temporary files and processing data may be removed automatically after processing or when they are no longer needed.

Cookies and local storage

The website may use browser storage for preferences such as language and theme settings. If third-party advertising or analytics services are enabled, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

Advertising

If advertising is displayed on VidSnatch, third-party advertising providers may process information needed to serve, measure, or personalize advertisements, subject to applicable law and your choices. Where required, VidSnatch will provide appropriate consent or privacy controls.

Third-party platforms

When you submit a URL from another platform, the request may involve that platform or service infrastructure. VidSnatch does not control the privacy practices of third-party platforms. Review the relevant platform's privacy policy for details about its own processing.

Data retention

VidSnatch aims to keep submitted media-processing information and temporary files only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide or secure the service. Retention can also be affected by hosting, security, and operational logs.

Your choices and requests

You may contact VidSnatch to ask questions about privacy or request information concerning personal data handled by the service, subject to applicable law and reasonable verification requirements.

Children

VidSnatch is not intended to collect personal information from children. Do not submit personal or sensitive information through the downloader.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the service, legal requirements, or advertising and analytics arrangements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to contact@vidsnatch.fun.
