FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What platforms does VidSnatch support?

VidSnatch is designed around a universal downloader for supported public URLs from platforms such as YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, Reddit, Threads, Pinterest and Snapchat. Actual availability can vary by platform and media.

How do I download a video?

Copy a supported public video URL, paste it into the universal VidSnatch input, click Fetch, choose an available quality and then click Download.

Is VidSnatch free?

Yes. VidSnatch provides the downloader interface without requiring an account or subscription.

Can I download private videos?

VidSnatch is intended for publicly accessible media. Private, restricted or unavailable content may not be downloadable.

Why does a particular video fail?

A platform may restrict access, the media may be private or unavailable, or the current platform extractor may not support that URL. VidSnatch cannot guarantee availability for every URL.

Is an account required?

No account is required to use the downloader interface.
