Technical support

When reporting a technical problem, include the affected VidSnatch page, a short description of the issue, and any non-sensitive error message that helps us understand what happened. Never send passwords, authentication tokens, or other sensitive information.

Copyright concerns

For copyright-related matters, please include the relevant URL and enough information for us to understand and review the claim. See the Copyright Policy for more details.

Privacy questions

For questions about data handling, cookies, or privacy requests, please see the Privacy Policy and contact us by email.
